# Team projects and presentations

## Your team directory

Create a subdirectory here named after your team. Something creative and unlikely to be duplicated by other teams (e.g., no "Team 1"):

```
session-2/projects/<your-team-name>/
```

Push everything into it: your prototype, any code, and your final presentation.

## The presentation

**Five minutes, including 1 or 2 minutes for questions.** Cover these three parts:

1. **The problem** you addressed
2. **How** you addressed it
3. **The result**: features, screenshots, prototype

## Using the template

Use the Google Slides [template](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/13cpxe0OfA7dH_6qfcF7PorIjqRIaZrWkLTPf-ET3ge8/edit?slide=id.p1#slide=id.p1). Please make your own copy, add this to your project folder, and push your changes to GitHub when you are ready.
The template is five slides with speaker notes on each, giving timing and what judges are listening for.

The grey italic lines are prompts — delete them as you go. The speaker notes are worth reading once before you start.

## Timing

| Slide | Content | Target |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Team, challenge, members | 15 sec |
| 2 | The problem | ~30 sec |
| 3 | How we addressed it | ~1 min |
| 4 | **The result** | ~2 min |
| 5 | Limits, and working with Claude | ~45 sec |

## What judges are scoring

The full [judging rubric](/judging/rubric.md) is worth two minutes of your time before you build the deck. In short:

- **Name a specific user and a specific moment.** "Clinicians need better tools" scores badly. "A scientist reviewing 30 candidates at MDT cannot tell which three changed since last time" scores well.
- **Show it working.** A demonstrated prototype beats a described one.
- **Every claim should trace to the data.** Do not put a number on screen you cannot point to a source for.
- **Say what Claude got wrong and how you caught it.** This is scored directly.

Technical complexity, framework choice and slide polish are not scored. Do not spend time on them.

## Before you present

- **Export a PDF of your deck into your team directory.** A PDF in the repo is a safe bet.
- **Put screenshots of your prototype on slide 4**, even if you plan to demo live. Something always breaks.
